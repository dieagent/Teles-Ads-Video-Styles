# Rain Myth style breakdown

**Basis:** Observed. **Reference:** 10. **Format:** Stylized 3D urban folklore. The screenshot sheet samples five points across the clip. The analysis is based on visible frames and captions; exact narration words and mix levels are not asserted.

## Visual system

Rainy blue-gray streets, simple round-headed 3D characters, dark umbrella silhouettes, red traffic light, a bold red creature-name card.

## Story grammar

Set a local legend in an ordinary street, reveal a threat, give the child a response, then name the creature and end on a small survival line.

## Motion and edit

Rain streaks throughout; lateral street travel, character turns and a short threat pose; title card holds over the action.

## Voice and sound direction

Campfire-style narrator, rain bed, light suspense pulse, clear separation between character action and creature reveal.

## Five-beat shape

1. Wide rainy setting
2. Threat under umbrella
3. Child reaction
4. Name and reveal
5. Survival or warning
