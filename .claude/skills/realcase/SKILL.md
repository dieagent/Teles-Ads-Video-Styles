## Style lock

- **Opening:** Begin with an ordinary person using an everyday product, then turn quickly to an unexpected medical consequence.
- **Image grammar:** Bright, smooth 3D reenactment: vehicle/drink, green-tinted distress, hospital, street vending machine, isolated bottle with vivid green liquid, warning sticker and shelf. Small white sentence-case captions low on frame.
- **Story grammar:** Victim and normal trip → sudden illness → death → other victims → poison mechanism → public warning → unresolved perpetrator.
- **Edit grammar:** Use direct chronological cuts. Make the bottle the recurring prop; move from a single hand to one isolated product to a shelf of many. Keep the product insert clear and clinical.
- **Sound grammar:** Tense neutral reporter voice, realistic atmosphere and a slight ominous pulse; do not make a sensational impact at the victim’s death.
- **Target:** approximately 30 s, 9:16, unless the spoken draft needs a different runtime.
