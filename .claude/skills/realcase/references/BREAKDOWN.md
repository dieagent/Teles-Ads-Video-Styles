# Real Case style breakdown

**Basis:** Observed. **Reference:** 9. **Format:** Realistic incident reconstruction. The screenshot sheet samples five points across the clip. The analysis is based on visible frames and captions; exact narration words and mix levels are not asserted.

## Visual system

Bright photo-real scenes: vehicle, hospital, street confrontation, clean blue product close-up, shelf of bottles; small white bottom captions.

## Story grammar

Tell an incident as cause, harm, response and object reveal, with every image tied to a sentence.

## Motion and edit

Frequent direct cuts, subject action in each scene, product hero close-up near the end; no animated diagram detour.

## Voice and sound direction

Straight news-like narrator, restrained room tone and street sounds, precise caption/voice alignment.

## Five-beat shape

1. Person and setting
2. Immediate consequence
3. Public reaction
4. Object close-up
5. Shelf or systemic scale

## Timing method

Use the final recorded voice duration as the edit clock. Put each visual on the word or phrase it illustrates; hold a picture only while the thought remains active. Plan an intentional sound-only pause only when the beat benefits from it. Test the first 5 seconds, a middle transition and the ending at phone size before full rendering.

## Boundaries

For real incidents, identify sources and avoid fabricated details or misleading dramatization.
