## What makes this video recognizable

1. **Hook:** Begin with an ordinary person using an everyday product, then turn quickly to an unexpected medical consequence.
2. **Visual identity:** Bright, smooth 3D reenactment: vehicle/drink, green-tinted distress, hospital, street vending machine, isolated bottle with vivid green liquid, warning sticker and shelf. Small white sentence-case captions low on frame.
3. **Narrative architecture:** Victim and normal trip → sudden illness → death → other victims → poison mechanism → public warning → unresolved perpetrator.
4. **Motion and transitions:** Use direct chronological cuts. Make the bottle the recurring prop; move from a single hand to one isolated product to a shelf of many. Keep the product insert clear and clinical.
5. **Voice and sound:** Tense neutral reporter voice, realistic atmosphere and a slight ominous pulse; do not make a sensational impact at the victim’s death.
