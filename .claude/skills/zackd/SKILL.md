## Style lock

- **Opening:** Start with a familiar modern comparison, then reveal a historical person who solved the same customer problem earlier.
- **Image grammar:** Stylized, cartoonlike 3D human scenes alternate with black and crimson voids. Giant white/red block type punches selected words; bright cyan sky fields open the beginning and promotional ending. An old catalog and product objects become hero props.
- **Story grammar:** Modern comparison → historical date/person → customer problem → simple new delivery mechanism → scale of products → business principle → clearly separate course CTA.
- **Edit grammar:** Cut between character reenactment and almost empty high-contrast type. Make products pop into isolated dark spaces; change visual mode on a major idea, not on every syllable.
- **Sound grammar:** Fast, conversational explainer narrator, hard hits on business turns, brief quiet before the business principle, brighter sales sound only in the outro.
- **Target:** approximately 38 s, 9:16, unless the spoken draft needs a different runtime.
