# Papertrail style breakdown

**Basis:** Observed. **Reference:** 2. **Format:** Editorial process explainer. The screenshot sheet samples five points across the clip. The analysis is based on visible frames and captions; exact narration words and mix levels are not asserted.

## Visual system

Warm cream paper, oversized black serif headline, rust red emphasis, travel postcard and cut-paper objects, tiny labeled step headers.

## Story grammar

Start with the visual goal, then reveal the production steps and a numerical result. The reference shows a style board, separated assets and final counts.

## Motion and edit

Objects slide and stack as paper layers; cards swap quickly while type holds; transitions feel like arranging a desk.

## Voice and sound direction

Measured explanatory voice; each on-screen step arrives with its spoken phrase; light paper/flick accents only at material changes.

## Five-beat shape

1. The result or hook
2. The look board
3. The asset creation step
4. Layers and assembly
5. Outcome shown as large figures
