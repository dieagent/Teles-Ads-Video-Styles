## What makes this video recognizable

1. **Hook:** Open with a compact cultural or material fact shown as an animated collage before explaining how that very clip was made.
2. **Visual identity:** Ivory paper field, black high-contrast serif headings, rust-red emphasis, Prussian blue waves and small cut-paper objects. Large circular color disks, postcard grids, code-card inserts and spare labels.
3. **Narrative architecture:** Illustrated fact hook → deny a one-click video method → reference moodboard → written style rules → individual scene art → separated layers → code motion → numerical result → comment CTA.
4. **Motion and transitions:** Use large centered step titles. Let the art samples stack, separate and slide; the typography stays steady long enough to read. The final result card is cleaner and more numerical than the early collage.
5. **Voice and sound:** Confident maker narration; short paper-slide and stamp cues at transitions, no distracting music under step labels.

## Adaptation rule

For a new topic, first show a short finished sample, then teach exactly how that sample was assembled. Keep claims about tools and cost tied to the actual project.

Do not promise a tool, cost or hand-keyframe count that the new project did not actually achieve.
