## Numbers on screen

- World Bank WDI current-dollar GDP per person: 1990 China **$318.5**, India **$371.1**; 2025 China **$13,862.0**, India **$2,702.5**. Ratio 2025 ≈ **5.13**. These exchange-rate-converted values are not adjusted for local prices. [R1]
- World Bank India Country Economic Memorandum: average **real GDP growth** in 2000–2019 was China **9.1%**, India **6.7%**. This measures each whole economy’s annual output growth, not per-person income. [R3]
- World Bank country comparison: 2025 **real GDP growth** China **5.0%**, India **7.6%**. These are one-year rates, not the historical total gap. [R6]

## Causal wording

China’s policy sequence and port/export link are described by R2 and R5. R3 describes India’s later reforms and service-led growth. The film’s “growth loop” is an inference from those sources and is shown as a mechanism, not a proof that a single policy caused everything. The jobs point is a structural concern, not a claim that India has created no manufacturing or service jobs. The generated city miniatures are illustrations, not archival footage.
