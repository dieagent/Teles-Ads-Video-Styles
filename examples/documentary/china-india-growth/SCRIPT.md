## Edit and narration notes

Do not turn “India down” into the answer. The visual reveal in B1 shows both countries rising. B2’s dollar figures are clearly labelled **current US dollars**, and B8/B9 use **annual real GDP growth**, a separate measure. Keep every graphic on the phrase that explains it. The World Bank page enters at the data claims, not as decorative wallpaper. The source reference’s chapter color progression becomes amber historical opening, green export mechanism, blue India services, red jobs gap, then warm answer. The narrator should sound curious and precise; avoid a victory or blame tone toward either country.
