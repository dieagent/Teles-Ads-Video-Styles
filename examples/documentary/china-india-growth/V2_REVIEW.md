# Reference comparison and second edit

## What the first cut missed

The [uploaded reference](../reference/snakebite-source.mp4) is a 127.37-second vertical explainer. A scene-difference pass found about 25 hard cuts at threshold 0.24, with more visual changes inside shots. Its three-second sample sheet moves among hand and snake close-ups, geography, taxonomy, anatomy, clock, bedroom, horse and vial. The source uses tactile modeled objects and purposeful chapter color changes. Text is occasional and short; the frame does not carry a permanent logo or citation footer.

The first China/India edit had 11 long beats and four reused miniature scene plates. Its camera movement and data graphics could not carry the visual variety of the reference. Its narration was also unnaturally quick. These are concrete differences, visible in the [old storyboard](renders/final-storyboard.jpg) beside the [reference sheet](../reference/deep-3s-sheet.jpg).

## What changed in V2

| Edit dimension | First cut | Second cut |
|---|---|---|
| Narration | 103-second fast synthetic read | 117.59-second read at normal Edge TTS rate, long silent joins trimmed |
| Cut map | 11 long beats | [35 voice-locked shots](shot_plan_v2.json), each with a purpose, asset, action and source ID |
| Scene inventory | Four reused wide plates | Ten additional original close, wide and object-led plates across the historical, factory, port, service and jobs chapters |
| Type and citation | Persistent brand/source strip plus large cards | Selective short headline cards; real data page inserted at the numeric proof beat |
| Movement | Slow plate push, paths and bars | Different camera targets plus farm sprouts, port lights, conveyor parcel, crane cue, export route, reform file, service cable, job gap and answer tokens |
| Sound | Voice, basic music | Normal-rate voice, quieter chapter harmonic bed and cut accents |

The [35 inspection frames](renders/) and [V2 contact sheet](renders/v2-storyboard.jpg) are included to make visual review possible without opening an editor. The [original World Bank capture](evidence/world-bank-data-page-tall.png) is retained separately from the designed insert. Data values, definitions and caveats are in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md).

The exported MP4 was decoded end to end: 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, H.264/AAC, 117.59 seconds. The director score validator passed. A silence scan of the narrator WAV found no gaps longer than 0.6 seconds at −38 dB. The sidecar captions were regenerated for the revised voice timing. The contact sheet and full-size data, port, jobs and closing frames were inspected; this is a visual and automated audio check, not a claim of a human voice-performance review.

## Remaining production limit

This cut is composited animation over illustrated plates. The reference has stronger native object and character motion, deeper camera travel and a more performed human delivery. A faithful next pass needs animated 3D scenes or video-generation shots directed from this score, plus a human voice performance. Do not describe this cut as visually identical to the source.
