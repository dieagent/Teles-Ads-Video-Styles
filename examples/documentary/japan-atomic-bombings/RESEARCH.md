## Fact boundaries used in this example

- The bombs were dropped on **two cities**, not on all of Japan. Hiroshima was August 6; Nagasaki was August 9. [R1]
- The US administration said it wanted Japan to surrender and to avoid a costly invasion. This is the **stated US rationale**, not a complete, uncontested account of motive. [R1, R2]
- The Soviet offensive against Japanese-held Manchuria was part of the same decisive period. The exact relative weight of bombs, Soviet entry and internal politics in surrender is debated. [R1, R2]
- Hiroshima's city estimate covers deaths by the end of 1945. Avoid a falsely exact combined death toll. [R3]
- A reenactment of the decision room or streets is an illustration, not archival footage. Put that distinction in the video description or end credits.
