# Director's script: The First American Mann Ki Baat

**Prompt:** `/director what if Modi became president of the USA`  
**Format:** 45-second fictional satire, 9:16  
**Chosen style:** `/zackd` / reference video 08. Its stylized 3D people, sudden red/black type cards and bright ending support a quick political alternate reality. This uses the **visual grammar**, while the new plot is original.  
**Opening label:** `FICTIONAL SATIRE · ALTERNATE UNIVERSE`  
**Voice:** Original, neutral comic narrator; no imitation of Modi's real voice.  
**Status:** Script and visual direction are ready. Timings below are estimates until a voice take is recorded.

## Story spine

A new president tries to address America through a one-way broadcast. His familiar greeting and hug diplomacy become increasingly elaborate, while the states keep trying to speak. He finally turns the microphone around. The last joke resolves the conflict.

## Production notes

- Art is stylized 3D caricature and graphic type. Create new models and sets; do not use reference frames or a photo-real fake news package.
- Caption each phrase on its actual word timing after voice recording. The background must change in response to the spoken idea.
- Keep the invented programme and presidency labeled as fiction in the first shot and description. The fictional title is not a real Modi programme.
- Check the joke with someone unfamiliar with “Mitron”: the phone reaction should make its meaning clear without a lecture.
