# The First American Mann Ki Baat

Premise: What if Narendra Modi became president of the USA?
Screen label: FICTIONAL SATIRE · ALTERNATE UNIVERSE
Target: 45 seconds, vertical unless the selected style specifies otherwise.
Style skill: .claude/skills/zackd/SKILL.md
Style reason: Reference 08 mixes stylized 3D character scenes with abrupt red/black kinetic words and a bright end card; those devices make the jokes visual while the new story stays original.

## Story
Goal: Address a new country with a confident first broadcast.
Obstacle: The audience and fifty states expect to speak back.
Turn: The competing questions overwhelm the one-way broadcast, so the microphone turns around.
Payoff: A fictional America Ki Baat becomes a two-way show.

### B2 — setup
Voice: Modi walks in, says Mitron, and half the press room checks its group chats.
Visual: Cartoonlike Modi figure enters; reporter phones light up; black/red keyword card.
Motion: Follow entrance, then quick phone-light cascade and reaction cuts.
Caption: MITRON / GROUP CHAT?

### B3 — escalation
Voice: At the summit, he greets every leader with a hug. The meeting clock does not survive.
Visual: 3D greeting line and giant clock; no real foreign leader likeness required.
Motion: Readable repeated greeting poses, then clock hands race.
Caption: THE MEETING CLOCK
Sound: Footsteps, quick clock ticks, restrained comic hit.
Purpose: Escalate the one-way performance physically.

### B4 — escalation
Voice: His cabinet brings fifty questions from fifty states. He brings one microphone.
Visual: Question cards stack in the 3D room; cut to a lone microphone on black.
Motion: Cards arrive in rhythm; hard isolate on microphone.
Caption: FIFTY QUESTIONS / ONE MIC
Sound: Paper hits; music ducks at one microphone.
Purpose: State the problem in one visual contrast.

### B5 — escalation
Voice: Then every state talks at once. Suddenly, the broadcast has no host.
Visual: Fifty stylized speech bubbles crowd a red void; podium shrinks.
Motion: Speech bubbles multiply, then one abrupt collapse to near black.
Caption: EVERY STATE AT ONCE
Sound: Brief layered chatter, hard cut to near-silence.
Purpose: Force the turning point.

### B6 — payoff
Voice: He turns the microphone around. For the first time, he listens.
Visual: Close hand turns microphone toward a diverse cartoon crowd; wide room settles.
Motion: One clear physical rotation, then slow pullback.
Caption: HE LISTENS
Sound: One mic click; music opens gently.
Purpose: Deliver the visual reversal.

### B7 — payoff
Voice: Episode two is America Ki Baat. The comments are open.
Visual: Bright cyan end card in reference-08 grammar; fictional show title and comment icon.
Motion: Title lands once; clean end cut.
Caption: AMERICA KI BAAT · FICTIONAL SATIRE
Sound: Short final hit, then stop.
Purpose: Pay off the running broadcast joke without an unrelated ad.
