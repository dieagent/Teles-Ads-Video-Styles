# Research ledger: What if Modi became president of the USA?

Checked 2026-11-06. This is an **alternate-universe satire**, not a claim that Narendra Modi is or can become U.S. president under current rules. Article II's eligibility rule is recorded below so the opening can label the fiction clearly.

## Joke selection

**Comic contradiction:** A leader known for a one-to-many broadcast enters a room where fifty states demand a turn. The story moves from his microphone to theirs.

1. **“Mitron” becomes a group chat.** Based on R3's public recognition of the word; the punch line is new and instantly visual.
2. **Hugs consume the summit.** Based on R4's documented public meme; the joke escalates the same communication theme through action.
3. **“Mann Ki Baat” becomes “America Ki Baat.”** Based on R1; the reversal turns a one-way programme into a two-way ending. The phrase is a fictional title, not a real programme.

**Rejected:** a yoga/curveball joke from R2 because it belongs to a different scene; generic “chai” jokes because they add a stereotype without helping this story; an imitation of Modi's real voice because the invented dialogue must remain clearly fictional.

## Story decision

Goal: address a new country. Obstacle: everyone expects to speak back. Escalation: the greeting, summit and press room all become one-way performances. Turn: the states interrupt. Payoff: he points the microphone toward them. The joke changes the story instead of stopping it.
